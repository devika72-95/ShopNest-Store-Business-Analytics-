# ShopNest Store – Business Insights

## 1. Project Overview

The ShopNest Store Business Analytics project analyzes e-commerce data to understand sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, delivery operations, payment preferences, product ratings, and seasonal trends.

The analysis was performed using Power BI, with interactive dashboard visuals and filters to support business decision-making.

## 2. Key Business Insights

### 2.1 Top 10 Product Categories by Sales

- Health & Beauty generated the highest sales among the product categories.
- Watches & Gifts and Bed Bath Table were also among the leading categories.
- These categories represent important contributors to overall sales performance.

**Business Recommendation:**  
Focus on inventory availability, promotional campaigns, and customer engagement for high-performing categories.

### 2.2 Delayed Orders by Product Category

- Delivery delays were observed across multiple product categories.
- Cama Mesa Banho and Beleza Saude recorded the highest delayed-order counts in the dashboard analysis.
- Delivery performance is an important factor to monitor across product categories.

**Business Recommendation:**  
Review logistics operations, identify recurring delivery issues, and improve coordination with sellers and delivery partners.

### 2.3 Monthly Delayed vs On-Time Orders

- The dashboard compares delayed and on-time orders across months.
- This comparison helps identify changes in delivery performance over time.
- Monthly monitoring can help identify periods where delivery operations need improvement.

**Business Recommendation:**  
Track monthly delivery performance and investigate the reasons behind increases in delayed orders.

### 2.4 Payment Method Analysis

- Credit cards represented the largest share of payment records.
- Boleto was another commonly used payment method.
- Voucher and debit card transactions represented smaller shares.

**Business Recommendation:**  
Maintain a smooth payment experience across popular payment methods while monitoring the performance of less frequently used options.

### 2.5 Top 10 Highest-Rated and Bottom 10 Lowest-Rated Products

- The dashboard identifies the ten highest-rated and ten lowest-rated products.
- Product ratings help understand customer satisfaction at the product level.
- Lower-rated products can highlight areas requiring further investigation.

**Business Recommendation:**  
Review customer feedback for low-rated products and work with sellers to improve product quality, descriptions, and customer experience.

### 2.6 State-Wise Sales Analysis

- São Paulo (SP) recorded the highest sales.
- Rio de Janeiro (RJ) and Minas Gerais (MG) were other major contributing states.
- Sales performance varied across customer locations.

**Business Recommendation:**  
Focus marketing campaigns and customer acquisition strategies on high-performing states while exploring growth opportunities in other regions.

### 2.7 Quarterly Seasonal Sales Analysis

- Quarterly analysis helps compare sales performance across different periods.
- 2018 Q2 recorded the highest quarterly item-price sales in the analyzed data.
- The dataset covers only part of 2018, so the yearly and quarterly comparisons should be interpreted with this limitation in mind.

**Business Recommendation:**  
Use quarterly sales trends to plan inventory, promotional campaigns, and seasonal business activities.

### 2.8 Revenue Analysis

- Total item-price sales were approximately 13.59 million.
- Freight charges were analyzed separately from item-price sales.
- The analysis helps understand sales performance and the contribution of different business segments.

**Business Recommendation:**  
Monitor item sales and freight charges separately to better understand sales performance and delivery-related costs.

## 3. Overall Business Recommendations

1. **Product Performance:** Prioritize high-performing product categories and monitor low-performing products.
2. **Delivery Optimization:** Investigate delayed orders and improve logistics coordination.
3. **Customer Satisfaction:** Use product ratings and customer feedback to identify improvement opportunities.
4. **Payment Experience:** Continue supporting popular payment methods and maintain a reliable checkout process.
5. **Regional Growth:** Focus on major sales-contributing states while identifying opportunities in other regions.
6. **Seasonal Planning:** Use quarterly trends to plan inventory and promotional activities.
7. **Sales Monitoring:** Track sales performance and freight charges separately.

## 4. Conclusion

The ShopNest Store Business Analytics project demonstrates how e-commerce data can be transformed into meaningful business insights using Power BI.

The dashboard provides an overview of product category performance, delivery operations, payment methods, customer ratings, geographical sales distribution, and quarterly trends.

These insights can help businesses monitor performance, identify operational challenges, improve customer satisfaction, and make data-driven decisions.

## 5. Important Data Interpretation Note

- Item-price sales represent the sum of product prices and do not include freight charges.
- Item-price sales should not be interpreted as profit.
- The dataset covers September 2016 to October 2018.
- The 2018 data is partial, ending on October 17, 2018.
- Dashboard findings are based on the available dataset and selected filters.
